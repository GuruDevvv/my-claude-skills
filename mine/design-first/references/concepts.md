# Concept variants — a bold idea grown from the topic's own objects

Mockup 6 of the six is a **concept** (mockups.md). In the first blind round of 25.09.2026 a
genre-only set lost on a work screen to bold ones — a day drawn as a **clock face**, a **dispatcher's
board**; across the rounds the user picked genre and concept takes about equally often, so the set
keeps both.

## What a good concept is

**A metaphor taken from the topic's own world that also does a job on the page.**

| Topic | Concept | What it does, not just shows |
|---|---|---|
| personal money | a till receipt of an ordinary month, printed line by line, total 0,00 ₽ | makes the leak visible — the spending *is* the content |
| therapist's day | the day as a clock face, sessions as arcs | answers "what's next and when" at a glance |
| therapist's day | a dispatcher's board: queue, waiting times, who owes | turns three lists into one control surface |
| robotics camp | an assembly manual: the robot snaps together as you scroll through the 10 days | the programme *is* the build |
| olympiad tracker | a season departures board | urgency is the structure |

Test for every concept, in this order:
1. **Is it from the topic?** The object/place/document already exists in the audience's world
   (receipt, clock, board, manual, diary, ticket, map of the venue). A metaphor imported from far
   outside ("a 1920s Berlin telegram") fails here — it made pages feel alien.
2. **Does it work?** Remove the metaphor — does the page lose information or an action? If it only
   decorates (a wheel, a chart used as ornament), drop it. That is what the user called
   «непрактичное творчество».
3. **Is it readable in 3 seconds?** The visitor must get what the page is about before they get
   the joke.

## How to invent one (5 minutes, not an hour)

1. List the **objects, documents and places** of the topic (from vibe.md read 4: topic props).
2. For each ask: "if the whole page *were* this thing, what would the main block be?"
3. Keep the one where the answer is the page's main content or main action.
4. Derive from it: layout (the structure of that thing), type (its voice — receipt → mono,
   manual → technical), palette (its real colours), motion (how that thing moves: printing,
   ticking, snapping together).

## Keep it inside the audience

A concept variant still carries one of the mood hypotheses and respects the audience read:
a clock face for a calm therapist is warm and quiet, not a racing dashboard; an assembly manual
for kids is bright paper and orange, not a dark engineering sheet.
