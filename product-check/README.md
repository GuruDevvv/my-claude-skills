# product-check

Whether the thing should be built at all — asked before the spec, not after the launch.

## When to use

The idea already exists and the open question is whether it is worth building: "is this feature
needed", "check this idea", "would this land". Also before writing a spec for a new product or a large
feature.

## How it runs

Five jobs-to-be-done questions: what job the customer is hiring this for, what they do about it today,
why they would switch, what stands in the way of adoption, and what number would show it worked.

Then a readiness score with the risks named and one concrete next step — which is often "go ask three
people" rather than "start building".

## Not for

Generating ideas from scratch (that is brainstorming), writing the spec itself, technical decisions,
bug fixes or small mechanical changes.
